About the Project

Each case study includes a problem statement, objectives, assumptions, algorithm, flowchart, pseudocode, Python source code, test cases, and conclusion, as provided in the assignment.

 Case Studies

1. Case 91: Advanced String-Based Registration Record Analyzer  
   Validates a registration ID and extracts the admission year, branch code, and roll number.

2. Case 86: Multi-Stage ATM Transaction Audit
   Processes deposits and withdrawals, checks transaction validity, and reports the final account balance.

3. Case 75: Integrated Student Record Consistency Checker
   Detects duplicate student IDs and marks outside the permitted range of 0–100.

4. Case 98: University Academic Progression Audit 
   Checks students' marks, attendance, and prerequisite completion to determine eligibility for the next semester.

5. Case 82: Multi-Criteria Internship Candidate Ranking 
   Filters eligible internship candidates and ranks them by their combined academic and assessment scores.

6. Case 70: Multi-Stage Scholarship Shortlisting  
   Selects scholarship candidates based on marks, attendance, and family income, then shortlists the top eligible students.

7. Case 65: Campus Resource Allocation Under Constraints 
   Calculates resource allocations and shortages without exceeding available stock.

8. Case 89: University Hostel Room Allocation  
   Assigns students to hostel rooms according to room capacities and identifies any students left unallocated.

9.Case 52: Hackathon Team Formation and Ranking
   Calculates teams' total scores from two rounds and ranks the teams from highest to lowest.

10. Case 79: Integrated Warehouse Order Prioritization
    Processes orders according to priority and reports fulfilled and outstanding quantities.
